Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 2026

Nitro Calls is committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and the exploitation of minors.

1. Prohibition of Child Exploitation

Nitro Calls strictly prohibits the following activities on our platform:

  • The transmission, sharing, or facilitation of any content that depicts the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Grooming behavior or any attempt to initiate inappropriate contact with minors.
  • Human trafficking or any form of child endangerment.

2. Reporting Mechanisms

Users who encounter any behavior or content that violates these safety standards are encouraged to report it immediately:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the "Report User" feature within the call or participant list.
  • Email: Send a detailed report to safety@nitrocalls.online or yusifliqezenfer90@gmail.com.
Emergency Reporting: If you believe a child is in immediate danger, please contact your local law enforcement authorities or international organizations such as NCMEC (CyberTipline) immediately.

3. Our Response to Violations

Upon receiving a report of child safety violations, Nitro Calls will:

  • Immediately terminate the account and ban the offending user.
  • Report the incident and all associated data to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and other relevant law enforcement agencies.
  • Preserve all necessary metadata for investigation purposes.
