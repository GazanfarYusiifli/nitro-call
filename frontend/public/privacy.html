Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Welcome to Nitro Calls. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide peer-to-peer video communication, Nitro Calls requires certain permissions and processes specific data:

  • Camera and Microphone: We require access to your device's camera and microphone to transmit video and audio during a call.
  • Network Data: We collect your IP address temporarily to establish peer-to-peer (WebRTC) connections through STUN/TURN servers.
  • Display Name: The username you provide is locally stored on your device and temporarily shared with users in the same room.

2. How We Use the Information

The information we collect is strictly used to facilitate real-time communication. We do not record, store, or monitor your video or audio streams. All media data is transmitted directly between peers (or relayed via our secure TURN servers if a direct connection is not possible) and is immediately discarded after the call ends.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party STUN and TURN servers (e.g., Google, Twilio, Metered) to help establish connections across different networks. These services only process your IP address to route traffic and do not access your media streams.

4. Data Security

Your video and audio streams are encrypted end-to-end using standard WebRTC security protocols (DTLS and SRTP). We implement industry-standard measures to protect any infrastructure data we handle.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: admin@nitrocalls.online
